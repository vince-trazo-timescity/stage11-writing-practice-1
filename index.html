Essay 1 • Position

Build a thesis that controls the essay

Question

Should volunteering be compulsory for secondary-school students?

Too broad

Volunteering has advantages and disadvantages.

Controlled thesis

Although school volunteering can develop empathy and practical skills, it should remain optional because compulsory service may disadvantage students with limited time or mobility.

British Council source • Model mining

How the model develops an opinion

Introduction

Restates the prison/community-service question and gives a qualified position: community service may be more beneficial in some cases.

Reason 1

Distinguishes violent from non-violent offending, then explains why prison may be costly and ineffective for people who are not a direct danger.

Reason 2

Argues that punishment should support reform; explains how community work may develop skills and empathy.

Counterargument

Acknowledges that harsh punishment may deter crime, then questions whether the available evidence supports that claim.

Conclusion

Synthesises the argument and calls for decisions based on evidence rather than adding a new main idea.

Adapted for classroom analysis from British Council LearnEnglish, An opinion essay (2019). The original task asks whether some non-violent offenders should complete community service instead of going to prison.
